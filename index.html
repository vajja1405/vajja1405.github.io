I build AI systems for the moments where the model's answer actually matters — flagging substance-use-disorder risk in patient narratives, catching dangerous drug interactions before they happen, and generating synthetic surgical video where real operating-room footage is scarce.

I finished my M.S. in Computer Science (AI emphasis) at the University of Missouri–Kansas City in May 2026, alongside a year as an AI/ML Engineer Intern at TIFIN, where my models ran in production for real financial advisors and investors. My work spans the full lifecycle: RAG architecture and agent orchestration, evaluation design (RAGAS, stratified benchmarking), and deployment on AWS SageMaker, GCP Vertex AI, and Docker.

What I care about most: systems that can show their work — every ClinIQ fact traces to a source URL, every model ships with an evaluation, and explainability (SHAP, Grad-CAM) isn't an afterthought.
